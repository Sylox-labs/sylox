# ADR-003: Coverage window and informed buying

Status: Accepted, spec v1.1 · Date: October 8, 2026

## Context

Two problems in v1.0:

1. **Informed buying.** `buy_cover` only checked staleness, band and whether an event was already in progress. A buyer who watched the public signals could buy cover while a depeg window was already running, endpoints were failing or the issuer had started clawing back, and before anyone proposed an event.
2. **The boundary.** Coverage was decided by `proposed_at` falling inside [start, expiry]. A depeg needs a full window of failing epochs before it can be proposed, so a 90 day series whose asset starts depegging on day 88 could only be proposed on day 91, after expiry, and the series would expire unpaid although the failure began during the term.

## Decision

- `buy_cover` is blocked if any of these holds (the cover gate):
  - any epoch in the trailing depeg window is below the depeg threshold;
  - the endpoint status was Down or Degraded in the trailing halt window;
  - any issuer action counted by IssuerFreeze (clawback or authorization revocation) occurred in the last 7 days;
  - any event for the asset is in progress.
- Because Depeg requires every epoch in its window to fail, this guarantees that any depeg window that triggers a payout started after the purchase.
- An event covers a series if its failure window starts inside [series.start, series.expiry]. Proposals are accepted up to one window length after expiry; the series stays Pending during that period.
- `EventRecord` gains `window_start`, defined per kind (start of the evaluated Depeg window, the epoch of the first counted issuer action, and so on).

## Consequences

- A buyer cannot buy cover on a failure that has already begun; any payout comes from a failure that started after the purchase, up to epochs not yet posted at the moment of purchase (bounded by the staleness rule).
- The day 88 of 90 case pays: the window starts on day 88, inside the term; the proposal on day 91 is inside the acceptance period (expiry plus 72 hours).
- A failure that starts after expiry is never covered, however long the acceptance period is.
- Every series spends one window length in Pending after expiry before it can expire, so sellers wait that long (72 hours for the default Depeg definition) for their collateral.
- `EventRegistry` gains a `cover_gate(asset)` read that evaluates all four checks from the ring buffer in one call; `Series` gains error `RecentFailureSignals`.
