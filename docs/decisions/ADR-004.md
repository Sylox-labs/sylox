# ADR-004: Contract inventory change

Status: Accepted, spec v1.1 · Date: October 8, 2026

## Context

In v1.0 money was spread across contracts whose main job was not custody: `EventRegistry` held proposal and challenge bonds, `RiskOracle` held signal dispute bonds, `ReporterStaking` held only reporter stake, keeper bonds had no clear home, and series sent protocol fees and slashed funds to "the treasury" although no treasury contract existed. Keeper and reporter rewards were paid "from the protocol fee pool" with no contract holding that pool.

## Decision

- Rename `ReporterStaking` to `Staking`. It holds keeper bonds, reporter stakes, signal dispute bonds, and event proposal and challenge bonds, and executes slashing and bond settlement on instruction from `RiskOracle` and `EventRegistry`. Refunds and winnings are credited to a claimable balance and paid by `claim`.
- Add a `Treasury` contract holding protocol fees, slashed funds, and the keeper and reporter reward pools, accounted in four buckets. Only `Staking` can accrue rewards; only governance can allocate between buckets or spend.
- `Series` holds only collateral and premiums. `RiskOracle` and `EventRegistry` hold no funds; they keep the dispute, proposal and challenge records and instruct `Staking`.
- The protocol is now seven contracts: `RiskOracle`, `EventRegistry`, `Staking`, `Treasury`, `MarketFactory`, `Series`, `Governor`.

## Consequences

- Custody is concentrated in three contracts with simple balance invariants: I3 (`Series`), I14 (`Staking`), I15 (`Treasury`), and I16 says `RiskOracle` and `EventRegistry` never hold USDC.
- The oracle and the registry become easier to upgrade and audit because they no longer move money.
- `Staking` is a new single point of custody for every bond; it is upgradeable only through the 14 day timelocked `Upgrade` action and must keep I14.
- The `reporter-staking` crate is renamed `staking`, and a new `treasury` stub crate is added. Deploy configs gain `staking` and `treasury` entries.
- Initialization signatures change: `EventRegistry.initialize` takes `staking` and `factory` instead of `usdc`, and `MarketFactory.initialize` takes `treasury`. The existing `event-registry` and `market-factory` stubs still have the v1.0 signatures and are updated when those contracts are implemented.
- Inventory (3.1), roles (Section 16), storage (Section 15), events (Section 13), errors (Section 14), API (Section 12) and the architecture diagrams (Sections 2 and 3.5) are updated.
- Sections touched: technical-doc.md 2, 3.1, 3.5, 4.5 (new), 5.3, 5.4, 5.6, 5.7, 7, 7.8 (new), 9.4, 10.2, 12.3, 12.7 (new), 13, 14, 15.1, 16, 17, 21.1, 22; `contracts/types` `BondKey`, `TreasuryBucket`, `Action::TreasuryAllocate`, `Action::TreasurySpend`.
