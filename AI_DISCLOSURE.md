# AI disclosure

Required by the SCF Open Track checklist (`prd.md` Section 15.1).

## How AI was used

- Drafting and iterating on `prd.md` and `technical-doc.md`.
- Scaffolding the initial repository structure and contract stubs in
  `contracts/` from the specification in `technical-doc.md`.

## How AI was not used

- No contract logic, economic parameters, or event definitions were
  generated without review against the specification documents.
- No AI-generated code has been deployed to mainnet.

## Human review

All design decisions (event definitions, manipulation defenses, governance
structure, budget) originate from and are reviewed by the Anchorline team.
AI-assisted code changes go through the same review process as any other
contribution before merging.
